📐 Methodological Foundations & Quantitative Connoisseurship

1. Chromatic Decoupling & Ductus Tensor: To eliminate sensor bias, warm halogen vs cold LED digitizations, and varnish yellowing, ArtStylometry extracts spatial luminance derivatives (Sobel operators and steerable filter banks) to evaluate directional ductus fluency and stroke orientation entropy independent of aging pigments.

2. Morphological Craquelure Masking: A black top-hat morphological filter isolates and masks high-frequency micro-crack networks (*craquelure*) and relining artifacts, preventing the model from grouping works by conservation condition rather than painterly touch.

3. Ledoit-Wolf Shrinkage Covariance & Mahalanobis Distance: Each master workshop is modeled as a multivariate Gaussian distribution in feature space. Because the number of canonical paintings is smaller than the feature dimension ($N \ll D$), we apply Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage to yield a well-conditioned, invertible covariance matrix for numerically stable Mahalanobis anomaly detection.

4. Conformal Prediction & Continuous 2D Attribution Heatmaps: Connoisseurship in visible light is treated as an empirical hypothesis test rather than a binary judicial verdict. Every canvas is assigned a conformal p-value and a sliding-window spatial heatmap revealing secondary hands, workshop assists, and modern overpaints.
